# Design System Documentation

## Theme Overview

This document outlines the core visual characteristics of our design system, derived from the current theme configuration.

## Colors

The design system operates in `light` mode.

### Palette

Our palette is carefully chosen to create a cohesive and branded experience:
- **Primary Color:** `#007bff` - This vibrant blue is the cornerstone of our brand, used for key actions and branding elements.
- **Secondary Color:** `#bac6db` - A subtle, desaturated blue provides support for secondary actions and less prominent UI components.
- **Tertiary Color:** `#00d2a6` - A striking teal serves as an accent, perfect for highlights, badges, and decorative touches.
- **Neutral Color:** `#83899b` - A balanced grey forms the foundation for backgrounds, surfaces, and non-chromatic elements.

## Typography

The typography in our system is designed for clarity and brand consistency.

### Font Families:
- **Headlines:** `publicSans`
- **Body Text:** `publicSans`
- **Labels:** `plusJakartaSans`

## Shape and Spacing

### Roundedness
Our UI elements feature a `moderate` level of roundedness (value: 2), striking a balance between sharpness and softness.

### Spacing
The design system employs `normal` spacing (value: 2), providing a comfortable and balanced amount of whitespace within and between UI components.
